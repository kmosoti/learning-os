## Citation Policy

Each chapter should use author-date citations in prose, for example `(Fan et al., 2023)`, and list full references here. If a claim depends on a particular source passage, add a note in the relevant chapter pointing to the exact section, page, or quoted phrase where possible.

## Evidence Status Notes

- The cited learning-science references support retrieval practice, spaced/distributed practice, explanatory questioning, and structured feedback.
- Knowledge Space Theory supports structurally modeling learner knowledge and readiness, but it does not directly validate the `learning-os` implementation.
- Fan's cognitive-tools research supports the theoretical value of externalizing hidden mental structure, but it does not directly validate skill towers, readiness gates, or failure-mode ledgers.
- Product claims about `learning-os` require separate validation with real learners.
